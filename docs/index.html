6 Annual Conferences

Want to run world-class international conferences that shape thousands of students and foster cultural exchange? Intrigued by the chance to innovate our programming to optimize for a digital landscape? Seeking professional development through our incredible network of alumni and Chinese connections? How about joining a family that cares for you in every way, from social activities to internship recruiting? Wherever your interests lie, you can find a home at HUAUSCR.

HSYLC

Held simultaneously in Beijing, Shanghai, Hangzhou, and Shenzhen, the HUAUSCR Summit for Young Leaders in China (HSYLC) is the most prominent educational conference in China reaching 1500+ high school students each summer.


Learn More!

CTB

China Thinks Big (CTB) encourages student research and social innovation through an integrated online and on-site competition that culminates in an international fair hosted on Harvard's campus.

Learn More!

YSA

Through Young Scholars Academic Journal (YSA), students can gain the rigour necessary for challenging themselves while en route to becoming published authors

Learn More!

BPC

The Business Pioneer Case Challenge (BPC) is the first real business case young analyst challenge for high school students authorized in collaboration with official content of Harvard Business Publishing.
